
THE STUDIO
Revised Product Requirements Document
v2.0  ·  Conflict-Resolved Edition

What changed and why
This document supersedes the original PRD and functional requirements. It resolves four documented conflicts between the brand aesthetic and the gamification scope, closes six critical gaps, and adds compliance and architectural guardrails. Every change is explained so the team understands not just what to build, but why.

1. Project Vision & Scope (Revised)
The Studio is a premium digital membership platform for a high-end barbershop. It serves two simultaneous purposes: brand perception (positioning the shop as a creative studio, not a transactional service) and member retention (a loyalty engine that rewards repeat visits).

DECISIONThe brand aesthetic is the container. Every gamification element must be rendered to fit inside it — not the reverse. If a feature cannot be presented in a minimalist, typographic way, it is either redesigned or deferred.
This decision was made to resolve the fundamental tension between the original PRD's 'zero visual clutter' directive and the functional requirements' badge grids, streak counters, and leaderboards. Both goals are achievable — but aesthetic must take precedence in every design dispute.

1.1 What We Are Building
– A public-facing landing page that functions as a brand statement and member entry point.
– A private Member Lounge where members track their status, complete challenges, and redeem rewards — all within a restrained, editorial UI.
– An admin Atelier Dashboard for reward management, point adjustments, and redemption validation.
– A points and tier engine backed by Firebase, with atomic transaction guarantees.

1.2 What Is Explicitly Out of Scope (v1)
– Booking system: The Studio does not build or own a booking platform in v1. Integration with a third-party scheduler is defined in Section 4.
– Native mobile app: The web app must work on mobile browsers; a native app is a v2 consideration.
– Multi-location: The architecture must not preclude multi-location in v2, but v1 is single-branch.
– Leaderboards: Deferred to v2. Public ranking creates social risk inconsistent with the premium brand.

3. Aesthetic & UX Framework (Revised)
3.1 The Non-Negotiable Design Rules
These rules apply to every screen in the product. They are not suggestions — they are constraints.

– Monochrome. Palette:
True Black (#1A1A1A), Paper White (#FAFAFA), Soft Slate (#6B6B6B). One accent: a warm, muted gold (#B8A070) used exclusively for tier status indicators and active CTAs. Never used decoratively.

– Two typefaces maximum. Typography:
Display Serif (Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond) for tier names, headings, and reward titles only. Wide-tracked Sans-Serif (Inter, tracking +0.06em) for all UI text, labels, and body copy. No mixing within a single UI element.

– Generous. Spacing:
Minimum 48px vertical rhythm between sections. Content width capped at 640px on member-facing screens. The whitespace is part of the product, not wasted space.

– Purposeful. Motion:
Framer Motion is used for three interactions only: page-level fade-in on initial load (400ms, ease-out), the tier progress arc animation on dashboard mount (800ms, spring), and the reward card reveal on scroll (200ms stagger per card). No other animations.

3.2 How Gamification Elements Are Rendered
Each gamification feature from the functional requirements is redesigned here to fit the Studio aesthetic:

TIER DISPLAYThe tier name is displayed in Display Serif at 32px. Below it, a single hairline arc (SVG, 1px stroke, gold fill) shows progress to the next tier. No percentage numbers. No point totals. The arc speaks for itself.
BADGESDisplayed as a horizontal 'collection' of minimal monogram-style marks — not colourful icons. Maximum 5 badges visible at once. Rare badges use the gold accent. The grid view is accessed via a secondary 'View Collection' link, not shown by default.
MISSIONSPresented as a clean checklist under the heading 'This Week.' Maximum 3 active missions visible at once. Completed missions are struck through with a single line and fade to 30% opacity. No progress bars on individual missions.
STREAKDisplayed as a number only: '4-week streak.' No fire icons. No animations. Placed in the activity section, not the hero.

4. Functional Requirements (Gap-Closed)
4.1 Booking Integration (Critical Gap — Resolved)
The points engine requires a verified service completion event. The Studio does not build a booking system in v1. The following integration model is required:

– The admin marks a service as 'Completed' in the Atelier Dashboard for a named member.
– This triggers the points award via a Firestore-backed Cloud Function.
– If a third-party booking system (e.g., Booksy, Fresha, Square Appointments) is adopted, it must expose a webhook that maps to the same Cloud Function.
– The integration point is documented in the Technical Architecture (separate document). The booking provider decision does not block frontend development.

NOTEManual completion marking by admin is the v1 default. It is intentionally simple. The webhook integration is designed but only activated when a booking platform is confirmed.
4.2 Points System
– Service completion (primary trigger): Points awarded by admin confirmation or booking webhook.
– First booking bonus: +50 points, awarded once per account lifetime.
– Birthday bonus: +100 points, awarded in the member's birthday month. Requires verified birth month, not full date (privacy).
– Review after service: +25 points. Validated by admin — no automated social scraping.
– Referral: +75 points to referrer when referee completes first paid service. +25 points to referee. Minimum 7-day delay before referee points unlock (anti-abuse).
– Spending threshold: +150 bonus points when cumulative spend in a rolling 90-day window exceeds the configured threshold (admin-settable, default: MYR 300).

REMOVEDSocial media check-in / share as a points trigger is removed from v1. See Section 2 conflict resolution.
4.3 Tier System
Five tiers. Tier names use the Studio vocabulary — aspirational, not gamified:

4.4 Voucher & Redemption Security (Critical Gap — Resolved)
– Every voucher has a unique UUID, a single-use flag, and a 30-day expiry by default (configurable per reward).
– On scan or ID entry, the server checks: (a) voucher exists, (b) belongs to the presenting member, (c) has not been redeemed, (d) has not expired.
– Redemption is a Firestore atomic transaction. The voucher status is updated to 'REDEEMED' with a timestamp and staff ID before any fulfillment action.
– QR codes are not static image files — they are generated server-side on each dashboard load and signed with a short-lived HMAC. A screenshot of a QR code expires with the session.

4.5 Referral Abuse Prevention (Critical Gap — Resolved)
– Email verification is required before any referral points are credited.
– A device fingerprint (browser fingerprinting via FingerprintJS) flags accounts created from the same device within 30 days.
– The referee must complete a paid service (not just register) before either party earns points.
– The 7-day unlock delay applies to both parties.
– Admin dashboard shows a 'Referral Risk' flag on accounts with 3+ referrals from the same device or IP subnet.

4.6 Points Expiry (Missing — Added)
– Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity (no points earned or redeemed) by default.
– Members receive an email warning 60 days and 14 days before expiry.
– Expiry window and warning schedule are configurable in admin settings.
– Expired points are logged in the fulfillment history for audit purposes — they are never silently deleted.

4.7 Account Recovery (Missing — Added)
– Password reset via email link (Firebase Auth built-in).
– If a member signed up via Google or Apple and loses access to that account, they can verify identity via email + last 4 digits of a recorded phone number and request manual account transfer by admin.
– Loyalty history, points, and tier are tied to the member UID — they survive an email change.

4.8 Notification Policy (Conflict — Resolved)
Push notifications are limited to three triggers only:
– Tier upgrade — sent immediately on upgrade event.
– Reward expiring soon — sent 7 days before a redeemed-but-unused voucher expires.
– Birthday reward — sent on the first day of the member's birthday month.

All other notifications (points earned, mission completed, new challenge) are delivered via a weekly digest email, sent every Monday. In-app notification banners are not used in v1 — the Member Lounge activity feed serves this purpose.

4.9 Reward Inventory Management (Missing — Added)
– Physical rewards (merchandise) have a stock count field. When stock reaches zero, the reward is automatically marked 'Unavailable' in the member-facing catalogue.
– Admin receives an in-dashboard alert when any physical reward has 3 or fewer units remaining.
– Members who attempt to redeem an out-of-stock reward see a 'Currently unavailable' state — no error, no friction, just a clear status.


5. Compliance (Critical Gap — Resolved)
5.1 PDPA Malaysia (Personal Data Protection Act 2010)
LEGAL REQUIREMENTThe Studio collects names, email addresses, birth months, purchase history, and device data. This is personal data under PDPA 2010. Compliance is not optional.
– A consent screen is shown on first registration, explicitly stating what data is collected, how it is used, and who it is shared with (Firebase / Google infrastructure only).
– Members can download their data as a JSON export from their profile settings.
– Members can delete their account and all associated data. Deletion is permanent within 30 days and irreversible.
– Device fingerprinting data (used for referral fraud detection) is hashed and retained for 90 days only.
– The privacy policy is linked from every page footer and from the consent screen. It is written in plain English and Bahasa Malaysia.

5.2 Data Minimisation
– Birthday: Only birth month and year are collected — not full date. This satisfies the birthday bonus trigger while reducing sensitivity.
– Phone number: Optional, used only for account recovery. Not shared or used for marketing.
– Social media: Not collected. The social check-in feature has been removed.

Appendix: Decision Log
Every major decision made in this document is recorded below with rationale, so future contributors understand intent.

– Decision: D-01: Leaderboards deferred to v2.
Public rankings are inconsistent with the premium, private-club aesthetic. They also create social risk (public shaming of low-engagement members). Revisit when the member base exceeds 500.

– Decision: D-02: Social check-in removed.
User-generated content is inherently off-brand. The Studio's visual identity cannot survive uncontrolled Instagram posts. Replace with a curated 'Styled by The Studio' gallery managed by the shop — a separate marketing initiative, not a product feature.

– Decision: D-03: Push limited to 3 types.
A luxury product should never feel like it is pestering its members. Three push types are the maximum that can be justified as high-signal. All others are opt-in email.

– Decision: D-04: Points not surfaced as the hero stat.
Points are a mechanism, not the brand. The member's tier is the brand. Points are visible in the activity log for transparency, but they are not the first thing a member sees. This is consistent with how Amex and high-end hotel programmes present membership.
